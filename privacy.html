Warranty Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Warranty Tracker helps you keep track of the things you own and when their warranties run out. This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, who else processes it on our behalf, and how you can see or delete it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Warranty Tracker is made by Tomi Laitinen, an independent developer (“we”, “us”), who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: warrantytracker-support@proton.me.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, used to sign you in and to contact you about your account.
  • Password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see it.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple: the details that provider shares with us, typically your name, email address, profile picture link and an account identifier. With Apple you can choose to hide your real email address.

What you put on file

  • Item details: item names, categories, purchase dates, prices and warranty periods.
  • Product photos you attach to your items.
  • Receipts you scan or attach, from photos or PDF documents; the app keeps each receipt as a PDF. Receipts can include personal details such as your name, address or the last digits of a payment card, so photos and receipts are stored privately: only your signed-in account can view them, through links that expire after an hour.

Scanning receipts

  • Scanning uses your phone's built-in document scanner: Google's ML Kit scanner on Android, Apple's VisionKit on iPhone. The scan is processed entirely on your device; we only receive the finished receipt when you save the item.
  • On Android, Google's scanner sends Google diagnostic information (such as your device model, Android and app version, performance and error details, and an identifier for this installation) to help it maintain the service. It does not send your scanned images.

Your plan

  • Whether you have Warranty Tracker Pro. Payment itself is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never receive your card details. Our purchase-management provider receives an identifier for your account and your purchase history so it can confirm what you've bought.

Technical data

  • Our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address, time and type of request) to keep the service secure and working.

Kept only on your device

  • Your sign-in session, and the schedule of warranty expiry reminders. Reminders are local notifications created on your device; they don't pass through our servers.
  • Copies of the photos and receipts you've viewed, so they open quickly and work offline. They're removed when you sign out or delete your account.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking, no advertising SDKs, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell or rent your personal data.

Who processes your data for us

We use a small number of providers, each bound by a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and storage of photos and receipts. Your data is hosted in the European Union.
  • Google: “Sign in with Google”, if you use it; Google Play for Android purchases; the document scanner on Android (see Scanning receipts).
  • Apple: “Sign in with Apple”, if you use it; the App Store for iOS purchases.
  • RevenueCat: confirms in-app purchases and keeps track of your Pro status.

Some of these providers are based in the United States or may access data from outside the EU. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data, items, photos and receipts for as long as you have an account. Deleting an item deletes its photo and receipt too. When you delete your account, all of it is deleted immediately. That covers your login, every item, every photo and receipt, and your categories. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period for security purposes. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies, partly because the law requires it.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap the account icon at the top of Your Items, then Delete Account. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account. Deleting your account doesn't refund a Pro purchase.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • correct it (you can edit your items in the app at any time);
  • erase it (see above);
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • data portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format.

To use any of these rights, email warrantytracker-support@proton.me. We'll reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live or work.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Every item, category, photo and receipt is locked to its owner at the database level, so no other account can read it, and photos and receipts are only ever served through short-lived private links.

Children

Warranty Tracker is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
