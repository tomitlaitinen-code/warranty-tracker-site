Warranty Tracker

Support

Get help

Warranty Tracker keeps the things you own on file, with their receipts, and reminds you before each warranty runs out. For questions, problems or feedback, email warrantytrackersupport@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few working days.

When reporting a problem, it helps to tell us your device model, whether you use iOS or Android, and what you were doing when it happened.

Pro and purchases

Warranty Tracker Pro is a one-time purchase that lifts the free plan's limits. It isn't a subscription, so there's nothing to cancel. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple for the App Store, or Google for Google Play.

Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your login (email address and any Google or Apple sign-in link),
  • every item on file, with its purchase details and warranty periods,
  • every product and receipt photo you've attached,
  • your categories and plan status.

This happens immediately and cannot be undone. Deleting your account doesn't refund a Pro purchase.

In the app

  1. Open Warranty Tracker and sign in.
  2. On Your Items, tap the account icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account, type DELETE, and tap Delete Account Permanently.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email warrantytrackersupport@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, using the subject “Delete my account”. We'll delete your account and all of its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Privacy

Read our privacy policy to see what data the app collects and how it's used. In short, there are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and your data is never sold.
